TROVIA Privacy Policy

Save. Store. Reuse. — private by design.

Privacy Policy Support

Effective date: November 10, 2026

The short version

TROVIA does not collect, sell or share your data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you save stays on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage that only you can access.

What TROVIA stores

TROVIA stores the items you choose to keep — text, links, codes, contacts, addresses, notes and snippets — along with your settings and the optional display name you enter. This data is stored locally on your device.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, TROVIA syncs your items between your iPhone, iPad and Mac using Apple's CloudKit private database. That data is stored in your iCloud account under Apple's privacy policy. The developer cannot see, access or recover it. You can turn off iCloud for TROVIA in your device's iCloud settings at any time.

Clipboard access

You decide what happens when you copy something, in Settings › Automatic Capture: Save automatically, Ask me or Off.

  • Mac: while TROVIA is running, it notices new text you copy and saves it according to your setting. Items marked as passwords or sensitive by password managers are skipped.
  • iPhone and iPad: TROVIA only checks the clipboard while the app is open. It never reads your clipboard in the background.

Clipboard content is processed entirely on your device and is never sent to the developer or any third party.

Sensitive content

Verification codes are hidden by default, are never shown in notifications, and can be deleted automatically after 15 minutes, 1 hour or 24 hours. When you copy a code on iPhone or iPad, it's placed on the clipboard for this device only and expires automatically.

Data collection and third parties

TROVIA contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and it does not use any servers operated by the developer. No personal data is collected, so none is sold or shared.

Your control

  • Delete any item, or clear your whole history in Settings › Data.
  • Export everything to a file in Settings › Data.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from that device. Synced data can be removed by deleting it in the app or in your iCloud storage settings.

Children

TROVIA is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions? Visit the TROVIA Support page.
