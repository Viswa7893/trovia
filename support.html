Frequently asked questions

Why doesn't TROVIA save what I copy on iPhone right away?

iOS doesn't allow apps to read the clipboard in the background. TROVIA saves new copies the moment you open it. To skip the system "Allow Paste" prompt, go to iPhone Settings › TROVIA › Paste from Other Apps and choose Allow.

How do I change what happens when I copy something?

Open Settings › Automatic Capture › "When I copy something" and choose Save automatically, Ask me or Off.

My items aren't syncing between devices.

Make sure every device is signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, and that iCloud Drive is enabled. Then pull down on the Home screen (or press ⌘R on Mac) to sync. Settings › Sync shows the current status.

How do I keep TROVIA running on my Mac?

Turn on Settings › Mac › Open at login. TROVIA keeps working in the menu bar even when its window is closed.

Does TROVIA save my passwords?

On Mac, items that password managers mark as sensitive are skipped. Verification codes are hidden by default and can be deleted automatically in Settings › Privacy.

How do I remove duplicates?

Open History. When duplicates are found, a card appears at the top — tap Merge to keep one copy of each, or Review to see them first.

How do I export or delete my data?

Go to Settings › Data to export everything to a file or clear your clipboard history. Deleted items are removed from all your synced devices.
